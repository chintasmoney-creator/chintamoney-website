FAQ

Straight answers

Do you give buy/sell tips?

No. Never. ChintasMoney gives zero trading calls, tips, or investment advice. It's a self-awareness and journaling tool that mirrors your behaviour — nothing more.

Do I need to connect my broker?

No. You log your trades yourself (or import a CSV). That's the point — your data, your mirror. No broker login, no market-data fees.

What is the Discipline Score?

A transparent 0–100 score built from your own trades: did you set a stop, follow your plan, trade on emotion, or overtrade? We show exactly how it's calculated.

Is my data safe?

In this version your data stays on your own device. Nothing is sold, and there are no ads.

Is this SEBI-registered advice?

No. It is not investment advice and makes no return claims. F&O trading is risky and most traders lose money.
